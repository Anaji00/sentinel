### A3 ownership breadth (trial, report only)
- Files: `shared/utils/breadth_change.py` (pure: point-in-time panel, 3-month change in positions, quintile spread, date-clustered bootstrap, rebalance dates, forward excess), `tests/test_breadth_change.py` (7 tests), `scripts/trial_13f_breadth.py` (reads `fund_holdings_monthly` and `daily_bars`, writes only `reports/trial_13f_breadth.json`).
- Note on the data: `fund_holdings_monthly` is N-PORT fund holdings (positions = funds holding the name), not 13F; the registration's "13F" label refers to this table.
- Choices fixed before the run (the registration leaves them open): tradable = close >= $5 and 20-session mean dollar volume >= $5m at the rebalance date; base >= 10 positions; newest month at most 135 days old; >= 100 ranked names; ties by ticker; the first public copy of each month is used, so later restatements are never read early; 76 rebalance dates every 21 sessions from 2020-07-01, so windows do not overlap.
- **Result (registered bar: |t| >= 2 in both halves, same sign, two-sided):** top minus bottom quintile, 21-session log excess return over SPY: mean +0.96 bps, t 0.04 over 76 dates, bootstrap 95% interval -52 to +54 bps, p 0.96; half 1 t -0.20, half 2 t +0.25 (signs differ). **Fails the bar by a wide margin. Not entered into the model.** The feature is dead as specified; the registration's alternative weighting (Choi, Jin and Yan) was not registered and is not run.
- Consumer: none by design (report only). Failure modes: a date with fewer than 100 names is counted in `skipped_dates` (0 here), not dropped silently. No model call, no Kafka, no Redis.
- Edge cases covered by tests: restated months, months not yet public, stale newest month, missing three-month base, tiny base, ties, too few names, opposite-sign halves, fewer than 6 dates.

## Proposals (all need owner approval or a registered test; none applied)

**P1. Read the 5-minute continuous aggregate in the signal engine series (needs a registered test).** Hypothesis: `tradfi_bars_5m` `close` equals `last(close, time)` per 5-minute bucket for every bar the signal study uses, so the study's reaction numbers do not move. Bar: on a 30-day sample of 500 tickers, at least 99.9% of buckets equal to the cent and every study cell's mean reaction unchanged to 0.1 bp; otherwise keep the raw query. Effect: removes most of 4,756 s a day of `_SERIES_SQL` time; no accuracy change expected. Cost: one replay script, no inference. Risk: the aggregate lags raw bars by its refresh window, so the live loop could read a bucket up to one refresh old: measure that before switching the live path (study path first). Also reconcile the 5,700 s a day of continuous-aggregate refresh `CALL` time with the 0.17 s job-run figure (addendum 6, item 1).

**P2. llm_news admission (needs owner approval: delays scores).** Trigger: a new headline. Dedupe: existing. Admission: send a batch when 10 headlines are waiting or the oldest has waited 30 minutes, whichever first (addendum 6, item 4: "scored 1, requests 1" repeated overnight). Consumer: the forward-only `llm_news_30` research feature. Bar: requests per scored headline falls by at least half at quiet hours; mean score latency under 35 minutes; scored headlines per day unchanged within 2%. Cost: none (reduces calls). Superseded entirely if the owner stops news scoring (decisions row 14).

**P3. Calibrated lower-bound score (needs a registered test, and a consumer first).** Per A5: shrinkage mean (`hier_bayes`) minus a date-blocked split-conformal half-width per cell, shown as the conservative bound. Hypothesis: ranking cells by the lower bound gives a higher out-of-sample hit rate over the next 60 sessions than ranking by the point estimate. Bar: paired date-clustered bootstrap p < 0.05 on at least 20 independent dates, and no change in the set of cells already passing G4 unless the bound removes them. Effect size unknown until run; cost: CPU only. Build only after the test passes, because today nothing downstream would read it.
