What the measurements corrected

  • The time-of-day clusters are crypto candles (30,123 of 40,663 events) and a daily short-volume batch at 17:00 ET, not the US session.
  • The prediction "0.300 floor" is a cap on quiet Kalshi trades that discards their score.
  • Earnings pre-announcement scores depend only on days to the report.

Wiring

Blocks only, in the enrichment service, behind SCORE_CALIBRATION_V2 (default 0, unset in the container). It lowers scores only, and 95% of blocks would fall below 0.6, so turning it on is the owner's decision.
